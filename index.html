Research question

How does explicit AI identity disclosure affect consumer responses to an AI influencer endorsement?

This is a CRM question because authenticity and social connection can shape trust in a brand, and trust may relate to purchase intention.

Design at a glance

One post, one changed signal

All participants saw the same sponsored post: synthetic AI influencer MIRA endorsing sneakers from fictitious brand NOVA. The randomized condition changed only the prominence of the AI-identity disclosure.

Speaker notes · 45 seconds

A clear summary for the room

This public dataset contains 600 Saudi consumers randomly assigned to see the same AI influencer advertisement, with or without an explicit AI identity disclosure. The disclosure was noticed as intended. Brand trust was modestly higher in the disclosed condition, while perceived authenticity, parasocial interaction and purchase intention did not differ significantly. This suggests transparency can support trust, but it does not automatically translate into stronger purchase intention. The evidence is specific to AI influencer endorsements and this sample.

What the evidence suggests

Separate the supported findings from the practical next step.

SUPPORTED · TRUST

Disclosure improved brand trust modestly

Trust averaged 4.35 with explicit disclosure versus 4.16 without it. The difference was statistically significant, with a small standardized effect.

p = .012 · d = .21
NOT SUPPORTED · PURCHASE

Purchase intention did not clearly change

The disclosed group averaged 4.11 versus 4.00 without disclosure. This difference is uncertain in this sample; it is not evidence of a reliable lift.

p = .178
CRM IMPLICATION · TEST

Measure the relationship, not disclosure alone

Track authenticity, connection and trust alongside intended action. Test the message and audience before making a broad policy from one stimulus.
